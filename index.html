About

At VelocityTX Innovation Center I've led records and data governance, co-led the organization's AI adoption program, and directed the build of an internal strategy-tracking web app. Before that I spent years in academic research at Texas A&M University–San Antonio, managing a cognitive psychology lab, supervising 15 research assistants, and teaching research methods and statistics.

The common thread is records and data governance, process standardization, and leading cross-functional work without a prior playbook. Much of my recent work is executed with AI tools (Claude and Claude Code) under my direction: I define the requirements, design the systems and data models, set verification standards, and make the judgment calls.
